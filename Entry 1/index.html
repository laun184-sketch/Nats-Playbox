The Central Question

What Makes a Puppet a Puppet?

Through this harmonic collection, I explored this question, allowing each website to reveal a different dimension of puppetry's essence.

Rather than answering all at once, I chose to break it down into its fundamental components. Each website serves as a chapter in a larger narrative, building upon the previous one to create a complete understanding.

  • Imagination Anything that you put your imagination into becomes part of the puppet. A puppet is not just a physical object—it's a vessel for creative thought and vision.
  • Storytelling Puppets exist to tell stories through characters. They are narrative devices that embody different personalities and perspectives in tangible form.
  • Interaction A puppet comes alive through relationships. The interactions between characters create dynamic exchanges that drive meaning forward.

Each website was carefully crafted to embody and explore its assigned concept. The design choices, interactions, and visual language were all intentional decisions meant to illuminate that particular aspect of puppetry.

By separating these elements across multiple websites, I could give each concept the space and attention it deserved, while creating connections that mirror how these aspects work together in actual puppetry.

  • Movement The physical act of movement is essential to puppetry. Without motion, a puppet remains static—it's through gesture and animation that the puppet truly breathes.
  • Story Development Finally, puppets exist within evolving narratives. Stories grow and develop over time, and the puppet serves as both witness and participant in this unfolding journey.
